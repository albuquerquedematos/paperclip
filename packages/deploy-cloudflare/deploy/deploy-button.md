## Deploy to Cloudflare

[![Deploy to Cloudflare](https://deploy.workers.cloudflare.com/button)](https://deploy.workers.cloudflare.com/?url=https://github.com/YOUR_FORK/paperclip)

Click the button above. Cloudflare will provision all resources (Workers, R2, KV, Durable Objects, Queues, Workflows). After approximately 2 minutes, visit `/setup` on your new deployment to configure secrets and create your first admin account.

See [doc/CLOUDFLARE.md](../../doc/CLOUDFLARE.md) for the full architecture and [deploy/post-deploy-setup.md](./post-deploy-setup.md) for step-by-step operator instructions.
